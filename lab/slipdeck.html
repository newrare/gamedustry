Slipdeck — jokers, no clock

One card at a time from a counted deck. Swipe left to bin it, right to keep it. A full hand scores chips × mult; high card, a pair under the floor or a cursed card busts. Jokers come down the stream: keep one to put it on the rail, bin it to refuse it. No timer — the deck is the clock.

Bench — does reading beat spamming?

same knobs, same joker pool, seeds 1–300 (restarts your run)
Always keep swipes right on everything, jokers and curses included. Line reader keeps what the hand can use (a held rank, a suit or straight line), bins curses and dead cards, keeps anyway when the chute is full, and takes every joker. A human should beat the reader; the reader has to beat the spammer by a wide margin, or the swipe is not a decision.

What to judge

Is there still pressure without a clock? The deck is the only clock: 52 cards, and a bin is a card no hand will ever get. With the anchor, a 5-card hand needs 4 keeps, so a deck pays at most ~12 hands — watch cards / hand. Around 6 means the player is choosing; under 5 they are keeping everything.

Does the pair floor kill the spam? Run the bench at pair floor any, then at J. Always-keep should collapse (deaths up, paid hands down) while the line reader holds. If it does not, lower shoe bias: the bias is what feeds a blind keeper.

Are the jokers read? A joker is a swipe like any card. Watch whether you refuse some — jokers refused at zero means every joker is simply good and the rail is a pile, not a build. The rule benders (Four fingers, Smeared, Shortcut) and the bin family (Golden bin, Ascetic, Recycler) are the ones that should change how you swipe; the Give buttons put one on the rail to try it straight away.

Does the tally carry the feel? The hand is counted out step by step — the category, each scoring card, each joker left to right — the way Balatro does it. tally step at 0 pays in one lump: play a few hands each way.

Are curses fair? A cursed card is drawn as one and busts any hand it ends in (Exorcist turns it into +20 mult). A veiled card hides its suit until it lands (Seer lifts it). Both exist to punish a keep made without reading; neither should feel like a trap that could not be seen.
