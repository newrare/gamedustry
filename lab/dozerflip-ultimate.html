Dozerflip ultimate

Half a minute of race, then the zone. Tricks build a combo and charge the super gauge; full, the dozer goes into fury and the city comes down. The park is lines: follow the chevrons from an exit to the next entry. Five letters and twelve gears are hidden on the heights, over the jumps and inside buildings.

Ambiance

Zone camera

Keys

W↑ gas · S↓ brake, reverse
AD steer (hard at speed: a drift)
Space jump · in the air: a trick
Space ×2 on the ground: dash
Space ×2 in the air: air dash (out of a pool)
In a pool: drive into the arrow to be thrown out
Gauge full: the next dash on the ground starts the fury
Race: each bonus is a push, and +0.1 to the multiplier of every point
In fury: hit the feet of a rail or a kicker to wreck it
The edge of the zone is an electric bumper
Shift dash · 1–4 ambiance · C zone camera
R restart the level · N skip the race

Touch

Hold and drag sideways to steer (the gas is on), tap to jump or trick, double tap to dash.
