Anything else it owns

Empty is not a file: it is a box with no picture in it, placed over a lamp the hub was already painted with. It carries a halo, a badge and a door like any other house and draws nothing of its own.

Cut but not adopted

A cut ships only once the game names it. Click one to adopt it — the role goes into art.objects and the cut is encoded, which takes a few seconds — then it moves up into the palettes above.

Shared cuts

Material no game owns, offered to all thirteen — the three cloud sheets are the first of it. Adopted by the same click and the same art.objects line, under the cut's own name (cloud-haze-03). A cloud is scenery, not a door: drop it high, size it wide and leave it on decor — a house standing lower is drawn in front of it on its own.
