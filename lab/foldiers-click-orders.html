Foldiers orders by click

Tap a blue region: its troops, pieces and actions come down as cards. Pick cards (the 4th infantry picks four), then tap a lit target: that is a convoy, three at most a region. Tap the region itself to Defend / Wait. Tap an arrow to change or cancel its convoy. A drag is the camera (wheel or pinch to zoom). Escape steps back.
